Make Your Dealership’s Website Like ChatGPT

Stop losing the leads that arrive on your dealership’s website and leave without talking to you. An AI sales agent captures more inquiries and shows you which vehicles shoppers are actually trying to find. Ideal for RV, car, and truck dealerships.

Your vehicle dealership website1–2% conversion rate. Not bad, or not enough?

In 2026, about 2.35% of sessions on e-commerce websites converted, meaning the visitor purchased, submitted a form, or completed another valuable action. Notably, first-time visitors convert far less often: just 1.0 to 2.0%.

If your dealership’s website is at even 2%, that’s good compared to the market. But even an improvement from 2% to 2.5% massively increases your vehicle sales — financing more deals, winning better-value trade-ins, and bringing in more customers overall.

If we, as an ad agency, made no plans to get you from 2% to 2.5%, 3%, and higher conversion rates, we’d be leaving that other 97–98% of traffic on the table. We’d be letting our clients lose money on their Google Ads, ChatGPT Ads, and everything else that drives visits.

What to do about it?

Answer shoppers’ questions before they leave.

Once a user has landed on your website, they’ve likely already cost you money: whether that’s paid ad clicks, all your SEO work on the website, or referrals you’ve worked hard to earn.

Think of each visit to the website as a cost. Each cost needs to give you back the maximum amount of revenue possible. Getting these visitors to book a test drive is immensely important in terms of making money — but also in cashing in on all the investments you’ve made over the years to even get these people to your website.

Enter the website sales agent.

A sales agent typically replies to questions from people on your website. For example, someone sees a Toyota Camry that just got listed on your site. The user shows up to view the vehicle’s details. But an annoying chatbot pops up and asks the user for their contact info.

The shopper might be looking for a Camry, but they decide they don’t want this particular trim. And so they leave. Did it help for a chatbot to ask them to hand over their contact info? No, not at all. What you needed to learn from this prospective buyer was: A) they’re interested in a Camry. Check — we know that because they’re on the Camry vehicle page. Next, you needed to learn B) that they want a Camry XLE or XSE, but not an LE. We don’t know that from this visit, so there’s little we can do to offer them the right vehicle unless they keep coming back over and over and hand you their phone number for a call. That’s unlikely: at a 2% conversion rate, the other 98% of site visitors won’t convert, and you’ll simply lose them.

Enter the AI-powered website sales agent

Why think a website chatbot with AI will do any better?

Here’s why: in 2026, customers are widely familiar with LLMs. ChatGPT offers a clean, quick entry point for shoppers to find information. 2026 estimates put ChatGPT users in the United States at 67.7 million (source). That’s an incredibly large portion of the population using one relatively new technology.

So there’s no question that consumers are familiar with chat-driven AI tools. In many cases, they get answers faster than from Google.

Imagine our Camry buyer has been going back and forth from Google Ads to all the dealership websites at the top of Google Search. They click into website A. They don’t see what they want. They go to website B, and so on.

Then they come to your website. They see a ChatGPT-like chat feature in the bottom right corner. They click it and start asking questions: “Do you have it in red?” “Do you have a lower-mileage model?” “Is there a leather trim option?”

A standard chatbot would have said “please give us your phone number.” But the AI-powered sales agent has collected all sorts of valuable questions. And when you have questions, you can provide answers.

“Yes,” the sales agent tells the shopper. “We have 3 other Camrys for sale. A red XSE with leather seats.”

“Great!” the shopper thinks. “I don’t have to go back to Google. I can just find what I need here.”

Even if you don’t have the Camry in red and XSE trim, the AI sales agent can be trained to notify your sales team of a customer looking for that particular model. In the meantime, it can tell the customer: “We have an XLE in blue with leather trim. If you’d like to see a red Camry, just give us your phone number and we’ll call you when we’ve found you a red one.”

Rather than opening with “please give us your phone number,” your website sales agent, all on its own, has discovered what the customer is looking for and collected their contact info. Now your sales team can follow up with exactly what the shopper wants, or make the call to suggest your closest alternative.

The end results, and the costs

Just a small improvement in your website’s conversion rate hugely improves your lead generation. An AI-powered sales agent is one arrow in your quiver of options. But it’s a highly effective arrow: shoppers get answers and alternate suggestions right away, and have fewer reasons to leave your website and go back to Google.

It’s also an extremely cost-effective option. A Business Development Representative typically requires $20-per-hour pay and only works from 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.

An AI sales agent won’t likely replace human sales reps altogether, but it does two important things. First, it allows dealerships that formerly could not afford a sales rep to answer website chat inquiries to now afford one. Second, it allows dealerships to collect shopper inquiries outside business hours.

AI sales agents are a new technology, so we don’t promise specific sales increases. But we do guarantee better info from your customers, more leads, and improved conversion rates of site traffic into real sales leads for your RV, car, or truck dealership.
