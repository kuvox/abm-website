broken tracking, item disapprovals, mis-allocated budget.

Common Problems Contact Us ›

Three problems Shopify stores experience in their Google Ads accounts.

Incomplete conversion tracking

There’s a high chance you’re missing conversion data. Google and Apple changes altered how most ad tracking systems work. You’re likely missing over 10% of conversions altogether. It’s a common problem!

Missing product data

You auto-exported your product catalog into Google Ads? Then you’re missing attributes and optimizations that keep your items approved in ads. This is the most common problem we see. And it’s often the hardest to fix.

Wasted ad spend

You have a keen eye for what your customers are searching for on the internet, right? Check your Search Terms report and find out if those are the searches you’re actually paying for. You might be surprised by what you see!

Your ads need attention. Attention takes time. Here’s what you can do next.

Contact Us
1

Have a glance at reports

Spend 30 minutes reviewing reports in Google Ads. It is common (yet unfortunate!) for shops to spend thousands on low ROI searches, while missing out on good ones!

2

Check how much of your product feed is approved

Shop owners are often dismayed to learn that 10-20% of their items are not even showing in ads. Why? Because they’re disapproved in Merchant Center.

3

Ask “did anyone update conversion tracking?”

Tracking is not as simple as enabling the Google & YouTube sales channel in Shopify. With enhanced tracking, you’ll see 10% to 20% more conversions.
